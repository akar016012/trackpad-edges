This is the first public version of Trackpad Edges.

- Slide along the edges of your trackpad to change the volume, the brightness, or your place in a video, and feel a tick on each step.
- Press a corner to mute, control media, open something, press keys, or run a shortcut.
- Give an edge a second job with two fingers, and give each app its own setup.
- A short tour helps you set everything up.

The app needs a Mac with Apple silicon and a Force Touch trackpad, running macOS 27.2 or later.
