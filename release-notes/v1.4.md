Corners in Trackpad Edges can now control your Mac itself and run your own commands.

- A corner can lock the screen, start the screen saver, turn the displays off, or put the Mac to sleep.
- A corner can log out, restart, or shut down the Mac, and it always asks you to confirm in a box first.
- A corner can toggle Dark Mode, toggle Do Not Disturb, or empty the Trash. Do Not Disturb needs a key that you assign in Keyboard settings.
- A corner can run a shell command in the background. You can stop a running command from the Corners pane or the menu bar. A failed command is reported in a notification with its last lines of error output, or in a short notice if notifications are off. A command that wants to control another app or press keys must be allowed on its own, and macOS asks under your shell's name, such as "zsh".
- The corner action popup is grouped under headers, so the longer list is easier to read.
- The diagnostics report says which way Lock Screen and Toggle Dark Mode use, and whether Do Not Disturb has a key. It never includes a command.

The first time a corner empties the Trash, macOS asks whether the app may control Finder, and it may ask the same the first time a corner logs out, restarts, or shuts down. The app also asks to show notifications when you first enter a command. These actions are new and have had limited testing, so please report anything that does not work as you expect.

If you use version 1.1 or later, this update can install itself.

The app needs a Mac with Apple silicon and a Force Touch trackpad, running macOS 13 or later.
