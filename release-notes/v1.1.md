Trackpad Edges now keeps itself up to date.

- The app checks for a new version every 24 hours, and it can install the update for you.
- You can turn the daily check off in the General pane, and Check for Updates in the menu bar checks right away.
- The menu bar menu has a new Release Notes item, which opens the notes for your version.
- The welcome tour ends with a link to the project, where you can give it a star.

This version needs one download by hand, because version 1.0 cannot update itself. Later versions arrive on their own.

The app needs a Mac with Apple silicon and a Force Touch trackpad, running macOS 27.2 or later.
